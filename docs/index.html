YouTube Two Channels Automation

An independent, owner-operated publishing tool for Vice City - Unlocked and Kidzz Rhythm. This is not a Google or YouTube product and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

What this app does

The app researches public video metadata for original topic ideas, generates scripts and narration, renders videos, and can upload and schedule videos on the two channels after the owner grants Google OAuth access. It can also add a first comment to the owner's GTA videos when the appropriate permission is granted. Made-for-kids videos do not receive comments.

The tool runs on GitHub Actions. It is a personal publishing integration; viewers of the channels do not sign in to this app. Only the channel owner authorizes account access. Some features remain in development and publication is subject to successful tests and YouTube's requirements.

Google account access

OAuth permissions are used to confirm the correct channel, read channel/video metadata, upload videos, and, for the GTA channel, manage first comments. Credentials are not collected through this website. Authorization occurs through Google's consent screen and a local desktop script.

Original content

Third-party videos are researched for ideas rather than copied. Any clip reuse requires appropriate rights to the footage and audio. Kidzz Rhythm produces original educational stories; singing features are still under development.
